About the Firm

A hometown firm with deep roots in agricultural law.

For decades, Lawler & Swanson has served Parkersburg and Butler County from our office on Coates Street. Clients come to us for careful, practical advice — whether they're buying a home, planning for the next generation of the family farm, or settling a loved one's estate.

  • Agricultural focusOur attorney is a past President of the American Agricultural Law Association.
