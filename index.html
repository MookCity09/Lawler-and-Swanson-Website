As a general practice firm, we handle the legal matters that matter most to rural Iowa households and businesses. Our fields of practice include, but are not limited to:

    Agricultural Law

    Practical legal guidance for farm families and agribusinesses — from land and leases to passing the operation to the next generation.Our attorney is a past President of the American Agricultural Law Association.
