About Amy

Amy K. Swanson was admitted to the Iowa Bar in 1994. Her practice centers on the legal needs of rural Iowa — agricultural law, wills, trusts and estate planning, probate, real estate, and taxation. She has served as President of the American Agricultural Law Association, a national organization of professionals working in agricultural law.
