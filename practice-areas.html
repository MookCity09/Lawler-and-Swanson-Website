Agricultural Law

Farming is a business, a way of life, and often a family legacy. We help farm families and agribusinesses navigate the legal side of agriculture with an understanding of how land, operations, and family relationships fit together.

  • Farm succession and transition planning
  • Farmland purchases, sales, and contracts
  • Cash rent and crop-share farm leases
  • Farm lease terminations and notices
  • Farm business entities and agreements
  • Agricultural tax planning

Our attorney is a past President of the American Agricultural Law Association. For general agricultural law and tax resources, Iowa State University's Center for Agricultural Law and Taxation (CALT) is an excellent starting point.

Call us about agricultural law

Wills, Trusts, Estate Planning & Probate

A thoughtful estate plan gives you peace of mind and spares your family uncertainty later. When a loved one passes, we guide personal representatives and trustees through each step of administration.

  • Wills and codicils
  • Revocable and irrevocable trusts
  • Beneficiary and ownership planning
  • Probate administration
  • Trust administration
  • Planning for farm and business assets

Our attorney is a member of the Iowa Academy of Trust and Estate Counsel.

Call us about estate planning or probate

Real Estate

Whether you're buying your first home, selling farmland, or transferring property within the family, we make sure the details are right and your interests are protected.

  • Residential and farmland transactions
  • Purchase agreements and real estate contracts
  • Deeds and property transfers
  • Abstract review and title opinions
  • Easements and boundary matters
  • Closings

Call us about a real estate matter

Elder Law

Planning ahead protects your independence and your family. We help older Iowans and their families prepare for health care decisions and the costs of long-term care.

  • Durable powers of attorney
  • Health care powers of attorney and living wills
  • Long-term care and Medicaid planning
  • Guardianships and conservatorships
  • Planning for a spouse or dependent family member

Call us about elder law

Taxation

Taxes touch nearly every legal decision, from selling land to settling an estate. We help clients plan ahead so the tax consequences of major decisions are understood before they happen.

  • Individual tax planning
  • Farm and agricultural tax issues
  • Estate and gift tax planning
  • Tax considerations in real estate sales
  • Tax aspects of business entities
  • Responding to tax notices

Call us about tax planning

Tax Returns

Our office prepares tax returns with the same care we bring to planning — and with the benefit of knowing the legal picture behind the numbers.

  • Individual income tax returns
  • Farm income tax returns
  • Estate and trust (fiduciary) returns
  • Gift tax returns

Call the office to schedule your appointment, especially ahead of the busy filing season.

Call us about tax return preparation
